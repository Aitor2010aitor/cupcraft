## What's new in v0.4.12.2

## What's new in v0.4.12

- **Keyframe diamonds**: Scale, Position X/Y, Rotation and Opacity have a ◆ button to animate them over time; preview and stage dragging follow the keyframes.
- **App language**: Spanish, English, French, German, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Japanese and Chinese in Settings.
- **Your own language**: export a JSON template with every text, translate it (by hand or with an AI) and import it to switch the whole app.
- **More complete translations**: panels, dialogs, inspector, timeline tooltips, toasts and effect/transition names now follow the chosen language (English fallback for any text a pack does not cover).

## What's new in v0.4.11.1

- **AI subtitle generator window**: pick the clip, language and a karaoke effect (animated previews), replace current subtitles, and see the Whisper model status.
- **Whisper download at the top** of the Subtitles panel.
- **Voice panel fixed**: always type text, choose a style and add karaoke subtitles; one button downloads Piper and a Spanish voice.
- **More karaoke effects + any color**: color, jump, big word, color box, box + jump, underline and progressive, each with an animated preview, and a color picker.
- **Fix: voices that failed with a phoneme error** (Amy, Joe, Ald...) now work.
- **More voices**: Spain Spanish (Davefx, Sharvard, Carlfm) and 3 Loquendo-style voice presets.
- **App language (Spanish / English)**: new selector in Settings; menus, buttons, tabs and most dialogs switch instantly and the choice is remembered.
- **More accurate subtitles**: Whisper Base model (re-download once, ~142 MB), cleaner audio, beam search and timing nudged earlier.
- **Text to speech marked Beta**.
